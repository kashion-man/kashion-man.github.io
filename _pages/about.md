Jiaxin Li
I am an undergraduate student interested in privacy-preserving machine learning and applied cryptography, particularly the intersection of federated learning and secure computation.

Research Interests
Federated Learning and Privacy: privacy risks, source inference, and secure aggregation.
Secure Multi-Party Computation (MPC): secret sharing, secure protocols, and efficient privacy-preserving computation.
Applied Cryptography: cryptographic techniques for secure and privacy-preserving machine learning systems.
Differential Privacy: privacy definitions and practical privacy-preserving learning.
Research Experience
SpecSIA — Source Inference in Federated Learning (Ongoing)
Exploring source inference in federated learning, including the effect of heterogeneous data distributions and approaches to evaluating the reliability of sample-source attribution.

Technical Interests
Python, PyTorch, federated learning experiments, and privacy-preserving computation.
