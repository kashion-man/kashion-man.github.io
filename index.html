Short Bio

Hi there! I am Jiaxin Li, an undergraduate student majoring in Cybersecurity at Wuhan University, China, and expect to graduate in June 2028. My academic advisor is Prof. Mang Ye.

My research interests focus on Federated Learning Security, Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning, Secure Multi-Party Computation, and Differential Privacy. I am particularly interested in privacy risks and secure computation in collaborative machine learning systems.

Feel free to contact me by email for academic discussions and collaborations.
