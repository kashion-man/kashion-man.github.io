Short Bio

Hi there! I am Jiaxin Li, an undergraduate student majoring in Cybersecurity at Wuhan University, China. I expect to graduate in June 2028, and my academic advisor is Prof. Mang Ye.

My research interests include Federated Learning Security, Privacy-Preserving Machine Learning, Secure Multi-Party Computation (MPC), and Differential Privacy. I am especially interested in privacy risks and secure computation in collaborative machine learning systems.

Feel free to contact me by email for academic discussions and collaborations.

🔬 Research Experience

SpecSIA — Reliable Source Inference in Federated Learning

2026–Present · Research project

Investigated passive source inference under an honest-but-curious server model. Designed round-anchored contribution attribution and explored adaptive spectral filtering to distinguish client-specific information from shared model knowledge. Evaluated attack success and stability in non-IID federated learning.
